Em dash remover and replacer

Replace em dashes (—) and en dashes (–) with a comma, a spaced hyphen or a double hyphen, and convert curly quotes and ellipses to plain characters. Each replacement is shown before you copy.

How the replacement works

  • Em dash between words (“results — as expected — were”) becomes a comma (“results, as expected, were”), a spaced hyphen or a double hyphen, as you choose in Advanced mode.
  • Ranges keep their meaning: an en dash between numbers (10–12, 2020–2024) becomes a plain hyphen (10-12), never a comma.
  • Quotes and ellipses “ ” ‘ ’ … become " ' and ... when the options are enabled.

Open Changes to review every replacement and click any one to keep the original dash.

Is the em dash a sign of AI?

No. The em dash is standard punctuation in English, used by writers and editors for centuries. Replacing dashes is a style choice; it does not reveal or hide whether a text was written with AI, and it does not affect statistical watermarks.

Style notes

English style guides use the em dash for breaks in a sentence, usually without spaces in American style and with spaces (often as an en dash) in British style. Italian and other languages often use a spaced en dash or parentheses. Pick the replacement that matches your house style.

Frequently asked questions

Why does ChatGPT use so many em dashes?

Language models learn punctuation from the texts they are trained on, where em dashes are common in edited prose. It is a stylistic habit, not a hidden marker.

Should I replace em dashes with commas or hyphens?

A comma works for most parenthetical breaks; a spaced hyphen keeps a stronger pause. Try both and check the Changes view.

Will this keep date ranges like 2020–2024 correct?

Yes. Dashes between numbers become a plain hyphen.

Does removing em dashes hide AI use?

No. It changes punctuation only.
