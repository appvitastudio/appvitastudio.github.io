Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026.

Who we are

Puliora is provided by AppVita Studio. Contact: appvitastudio@proton.me.

Website

  • Text and files you paste or open are processed only in your browser. The pages block network requests from scripts with a Content Security Policy (connect-src 'none'), and the background worker used for long texts and files disables its network functions when it starts.
  • We use no cookies, no analytics and no advertising on the website.
  • Nothing is stored on your device unless you choose to: the theme and language preference, custom rules when you press Save, and the optional local history (dates and counts, never text). You can delete them from Settings or by clearing the site data in your browser.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Android app

  • Text you paste, share to the app or select with “Puliora” is processed only on your phone and is never sent to us or to third parties.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and usage and diagnostic data to deliver, measure and personalise ads and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent tool (UMP) and can change your choice at any time from the app's privacy options. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • The app requests the Internet permission only for ads.

Chrome extension

The extension reads the selected text of a tab only when you invoke it, and the clipboard only when you click Paste & clean clipboard in its popup. It sends nothing over the network and stores only your settings.

API

The API is not available yet. When it is, texts will be processed in memory to produce the response and not stored or logged; this policy will be updated before launch.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access, rectification or deletion of personal data. We do not hold personal data from the website or the app; for data processed by Google for ads, use Google's tools or contact us.

Children

Puliora is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when this policy changes.
