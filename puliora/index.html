What Puliora finds

Invisible characters

Zero-width spaces and joiners, word joiners, byte-order marks, soft hyphens, invisible math operators, blank fillers.

Spaces that are not spaces

No-break spaces, the narrow no-break space U+202F seen in chatbot output, en/em/thin spaces, non-breaking hyphens.

Hidden messages

Text written with Unicode tag characters, data smuggled in variation selectors, zero-width steganography — decoded and shown.

Direction tricks

Bidirectional overrides and isolates that make text (or code) display in a different order than it is stored.

Look-alike letters

Cyrillic or Greek letters inside Latin words, full-width letters and styled “fancy text” letters.

Copy-paste damage

Control characters, terminal colour codes, broken characters, mojibake like “â€™”, PDF line breaks and ligatures.

What it never touches

Many “invisible” characters are part of correct text. Puliora uses the official Unicode data to recognise their context and keeps them:

  • the zero-width joiner inside emoji such as 👩‍💻 or 👨‍👩‍👧, and variation selectors such as the one in ❤️;
  • the zero-width non-joiner in Persian (می‌خواهم) and the joiners of Hindi, Bengali, Malayalam, Sinhala and other Indic scripts;
  • direction marks in Arabic and Hebrew text;
  • no-break spaces used on purpose: number grouping (10 000) and French punctuation (« Bonjour ! »).

Every change is listed in Changes; click any change to skip it. Nothing replaces your original text unless you choose Use as original, and Undo is always available.

Private by design

The cleaner runs entirely in your browser. This page declares a Content Security Policy with connect-src 'none': the browser itself refuses any network request from the page's scripts. Long texts are cleaned in a background worker, which the page's policy does not cover, so the worker disables fetch, XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket, EventSource and importScripts as soon as it starts, and the cleaning code itself makes no network requests: your text is not sent anywhere. You can check it in your browser's developer tools (Network tab) while you paste. There is no account and no analytics on your content.

What no character cleaner can do

Some AI providers embed statistical watermarks in the choice of words (for example Google's SynthID Text, and the text watermarks announced by Anthropic in August 2026 and by OpenAI in October 2026). Those signals contain no special characters, so removing characters does not touch them. Puliora also cannot tell who wrote a text: a text without invisible characters is not necessarily human. Frequently asked questions

Does ChatGPT add hidden characters?

Not as a deliberate watermark, as far as public information shows. In 2025 some OpenAI models were reported to output narrow no-break spaces (U+202F) instead of normal spaces; OpenAI reportedly described it as a side effect of training. Text copied from any chatbot, website or document can also contain zero-width spaces, no-break spaces and other artifacts. Puliora finds them whatever their origin.

Will cleaning change an AI detector's result?

You should not expect it to. AI detectors judge the style and wording of a text, and statistical watermarks live in the choice of words. Removing characters changes neither. Puliora is a text-quality tool, not a way to hide AI use.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens on your device: the page blocks network requests with its Content Security Policy, and the background worker used for long texts has its network functions disabled.

Will it break emoji or Arabic, Persian or Hindi text?

No. Joiners and selectors that are part of emoji sequences or of the spelling of a script are recognised and kept. The test suite checks dozens of scripts and emoji sequences.

What is the difference between Safe and Strict?

Safe removes only characters with no function in their context. Strict also normalises typographic no-break spaces, full-width and styled letters, ligatures and look-alike letters in mixed-script words. Advanced mode lets you choose per category.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. Click a change in the Changes view to skip it, use Undo/Redo, or Restore original. The JSON report contains a change log that reconstructs the original exactly.
