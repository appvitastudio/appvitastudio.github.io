# DON'T LOOK - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026
App: DON'T LOOK (Android) - package `com.appvitastudio.dontlook`
Data controller: AppVita Studio - contact: appvitastudio@proton.me

## Short version

DON'T LOOK is a game that runs entirely on your phone. There is no account, no sign-up and
no server of ours. We do not receive, read or store anything about you or how you play.
The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising SDK (AdMob) needs in
order to show ads.

## What stays on your device

The game writes a small save file to the storage of your phone. It contains:

- your settings: language, camera sensitivity, music and sound volume, difficulty;
- your statistics: runs played, best score, longest streak, anomalies found;
- which anomalies you have already met, for the in-game codex;
- the date of your last Daily Challenge attempt, so the game knows it is one per day;
- whether an ad-free period earned with a rewarded video is still running.

There is no cloud sync, no login, no leaderboard and no analytics SDK. Nothing in that file
identifies you. If you uninstall the app, it is deleted with it.

## Advertising (AdMob)

DON'T LOOK is free and is paid for by ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google's SDK
may collect and process:

- the advertising identifier of the device (Advertising ID);
- approximate, coarse information derived from the IP address (such as country);
- technical information about the device and the app (model, operating system version, app
version, language);
- interactions with the ads themselves (impressions, clicks).

This processing is done by Google as an independent controller. We do not receive this
data, and we do not combine it with anything the game stores on your phone. Google's own
privacy policy applies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time from the Android settings
(Settings > Privacy > Ads), which limits ad personalisation.

No ad is ever shown during a run. Ads appear only in the menus and between runs. If you
watch a rewarded video by your own choice, banners and full-screen ads are switched off
for the following thirty minutes.

## Permissions

DON'T LOOK asks for the minimum it needs:

- `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` - to load ads;
- `AD_ID` - the advertising identifier used by AdMob.

The game does not ask for location, contacts, microphone, camera or file access, and it
works offline apart from the ads.

## Children

DON'T LOOK is a horror game and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect
any data from children.

## Your rights

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or
delete: everything is on your phone and under your control (clear the app data, or
uninstall the app). For the data processed by Google for advertising, please use Google's
own controls linked above.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the legal
basis for the advertising processing is your consent where required, and legitimate
interest for non-personalised advertising and for the security of the service.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated. Material changes will
also be described in the release notes of the app on Google Play.
