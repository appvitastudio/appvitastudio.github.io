# The Last Message - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026
App: The Last Message (Android) - package `com.appvitastudio.lastmessage`
Data controller: AppVita Studio - contact: appvitastudio@proton.me

## Short version

The Last Message is a story game played on a fictional phone. The phone, its contacts,
messages, photos and calls are all part of the story. The game never reads, uses or uploads
your real contacts, messages, photos, camera, microphone, call log or location.

There is no account, no sign-up and no server of ours. We do not receive, read or store
anything about you or how you play. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's
advertising SDK (AdMob) needs in order to show ads.

## What stays on your device

The game writes a small save file to the private storage of your phone. It contains:

- your progress: current chapter, choices, clues found, endings and achievements;
- the name you typed for your character;
- your settings: language, music and sound volume, vibration, text size and speed,
  accessibility options;
- whether an ad-free period earned with a rewarded video is still running.

There is no cloud sync, no login, no leaderboard and no analytics SDK. Nothing in that file
identifies you. If you uninstall the app, it is deleted with it. You can also erase it at any
time from Settings > Erase all progress.

## Advertising (AdMob)

The Last Message is free and is paid for by ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google's
SDK may collect and process:

- the advertising identifier of the device (Advertising ID);
- approximate, coarse information derived from the IP address (such as country);
- technical information about the device and the app (model, operating system version, app
  version, language);
- interactions with the ads themselves (impressions, clicks).

This processing is done by Google as an independent controller. We do not receive this data,
and we do not combine it with anything the game stores on your phone. Google's own privacy
policy applies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for
your consent before personalised ads are shown (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change
your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your
Advertising ID from the Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

No ad is ever shown during a chapter. Ads appear only in the menus and between chapters. Rewarded
videos are always optional and always your choice.

## Permissions

The Last Message asks for the minimum it needs:

- `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` - to load ads;
- `AD_ID` - the advertising identifier used by AdMob;
- `VIBRATE` - for the phone's vibration during the story (can be switched off in Settings).

The game does not ask for location, contacts, SMS, call log, microphone, camera or file access,
and the whole story works offline.

## Children

The Last Message is a psychological horror game and is not directed to children. It is intended
for players aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

## Your rights

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or
delete: everything is on your phone and under your control (erase your progress in the game,
clear the app data, or uninstall the app). For the data processed by Google for advertising,
please use Google's own controls linked above.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the legal basis for
the advertising processing is your consent where required, and legitimate interest for
non-personalised advertising and for the security of the service.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated. Material changes will also
be described in the release notes of the app on Google Play.

# The Last Message - Informativa sulla privacy (italiano)

Ultimo aggiornamento: 23 settembre 2026

The Last Message e' un gioco narrativo ambientato in un telefono immaginario: contatti, messaggi,
foto e chiamate fanno parte della storia. Il gioco non legge, non usa e non invia mai i tuoi veri
contatti, messaggi, foto, fotocamera, microfono, registro chiamate o la tua posizione.

- **Sul dispositivo**: progressi, nome del personaggio e impostazioni sono salvati solo nella
  memoria privata del gioco e non ci vengono mai inviati. Si cancellano disinstallando il gioco o
  da Impostazioni > Cancella tutti i progressi.
- **Pubblicita'**: il gioco e' gratuito e mostra annunci tramite Google AdMob, che puo' trattare
  l'ID pubblicita', informazioni tecniche sul dispositivo, la posizione approssimativa ricavata
  dall'indirizzo IP e le interazioni con gli annunci, secondo la Norma sulla privacy di Google
  (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Nello SEE, nel Regno Unito e in Svizzera il gioco chiede
  il consenso prima degli annunci personalizzati; la scelta si cambia da Impostazioni > Opzioni
  privacy. Gli annunci non compaiono mai durante un capitolo.
- **Cosa non facciamo**: nessun account, nessun server nostro, nessuna vendita di dati, nessun SDK
  di analisi. Il gioco non e' destinato ai minori di 16 anni.
- **Contatti**: appvitastudio@proton.me
