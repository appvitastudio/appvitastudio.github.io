# Privacy Policy - Astrora

**Last updated: 6 October 2026**

Astrora ("the game") is a retro space shooter game published by **AppVita Studio**. This policy
explains what data the game handles and why.

## 1. Your progress stays on your device

Your game data - campaign progress and stars, best scores, arcade and daily challenge
results, coins, ship upgrades in the hangar, daily rewards and your settings (language,
sound, music, vibration) - is stored **only on your device**, in the game's private storage.

We do not collect it, we do not transmit it, we do not share it and we have no servers
that receive it. There is no account and no registration, so there is no account to delete.
The game works fully offline.

If you use the **share** button, the game opens the Android share sheet with a short text
about your result: it goes only where you choose to send it.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Astrora is free and is supported by advertising provided by **Google AdMob**:

- a banner on some screens
- an occasional full-screen ad between games (never in the middle of a game)
- optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch to get a bonus (for example a hint,
  a revive, a boost before a level, double rewards or free coins)

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and
  **approximate location derived from your IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller, according to
its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

**Your game progress is never shared with the advertising network.**

## 3. Consent (EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows
you a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) **before any ad is loaded**.
You choose whether to allow personalised advertising.

If consent is not given, no ads are requested at all.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the **Privacy options** button in
the game's settings.

## 4. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise
location, or your game progress. The game does not request any special permission (no
location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage) and uses no analytics SDK of its own.

## 5. Children

The game is not directed to children. It is intended for users aged 18 and over.

## 6. Your rights

Since your data never leaves your device, you are in full control of it:

- **Deletion**: clear the app data from the Android settings, or uninstall the game to
  remove everything
- **Advertising**: reset or delete your advertising ID from the Android settings, and
  change your consent from the Privacy options button in the game

For anything concerning advertising data processed by Google, you can exercise your rights
directly with Google through the links in section 2.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page changes with it, and
the new version is published at this same address.
