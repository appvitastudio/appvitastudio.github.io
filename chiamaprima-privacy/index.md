# Privacy Policy - Chiama Prima

**Last updated: 13 October 2026**

Chiama Prima ("the app") helps you and your family stop and call a trusted person before paying,
sharing codes or opening the door to strangers. It is published by **AppVita Studio**. This policy
explains what data the app handles and why.

## 1. Your data stays on your device

The trusted contacts you add (name and phone number you type in), your checklists, notes and
settings (language, preferences) are stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage.

We do not collect them, we do not transmit them, we do not share them and we have no servers that
receive them. There is no account and no registration, so there is no account to delete.

When you tap "call", the app opens your phone's dialer with the number you saved: the call is made
by your phone, not by the app, and we never see who you call.

The app does not read your address book and does not request permission to access your contacts,
call log, location, camera or microphone.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Chiama Prima is free and is supported by a small banner provided by **Google AdMob**.

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and
  **approximate location derived from your IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller, according to its own
policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

**Your contacts and notes are never shared with the advertising network.**

## 3. Consent (EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows you a
consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) **before any ad is loaded**. You choose
whether to allow personalised advertising.

If consent is not given, no ads are requested at all.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the **Privacy options** button in the app's
settings.

## 4. Children

The app is not directed to children. It is intended for adults who want to protect themselves and
their family from scams.

## 5. Your rights

Since your data never leaves your device, you are in full control of it:

- **Deletion**: delete contacts inside the app, clear the app data from the Android settings, or
  uninstall the app to remove everything
- **Advertising**: reset or delete your advertising ID from the Android settings, and change your
  consent from the Privacy options button in the app
