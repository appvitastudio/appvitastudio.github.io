# Don't Touch - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026
App: Don't Touch (Android) - package `com.appvitastudio.donttouch`
Data controller: AppVita Studio - contact: appvitastudio@proton.me

**Don't Touch** ("the game") is a puzzle game. This policy explains what data
the game handles.

## Data the game stores on your device
Your progress (levels, stars, records, achievements), your settings (sound,
language, theme) and the result of each Daily challenge are saved **only on
your device**. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

The game has no account, no login, no chat, and does not ask for access to
contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The game is free and shows ads through **Google AdMob**. To show and measure
ads, Google may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP
address, device and app information and interactions with ads. In the
European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for
consent before personalised ads are shown (Google User Messaging Platform),
and you can change your choice at any time from **Settings > Ad privacy
choices** in the game.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children
The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children
under 13. Ads are requested with Google's standard settings.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address.
