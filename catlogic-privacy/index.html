Cat Logic: Pawprint Puzzles — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Developer: AppVita Studio · Contact: appvitastudio@proton.me

English

The game itself collects no personal data. Progress, settings and statistics are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. The game works fully offline.

Advertising. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (banner on menus, occasional interstitial between puzzles, optional rewarded videos). Google may collect and process device identifiers (advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and usage/diagnostic data to serve, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. In the EEA/UK/Switzerland consent is requested through Google's consent form (UMP) before ads are requested, and can be changed at any time from Settings → Privacy options. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Sharing. When you tap "Share", an image is created on your device and handed to the Android share sheet; nothing is uploaded by the game.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13.

Deletion. Uninstalling the app or "Reset progress" deletes all local data. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Italiano

Il gioco non raccoglie dati personali. Progressi, impostazioni e statistiche restano solo sul dispositivo e funziona completamente offline. La versione gratuita mostra annunci di Google AdMob, che può trattare identificativi del dispositivo (ID pubblicità), indirizzo IP, posizione approssimativa e dati di utilizzo per pubblicare e misurare gli annunci. Nello SEE/Regno Unito/Svizzera il consenso viene chiesto con il modulo di Google prima di richiedere annunci ed è modificabile da Impostazioni → Opzioni privacy. Disinstallare l'app o "Azzera progressi" elimina tutti i dati locali. Contatto: appvitastudio@proton.me
