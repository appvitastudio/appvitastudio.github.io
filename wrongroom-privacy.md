# The Wrong Room - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026
App: The Wrong Room (Android) - package `com.appvitastudio.wrongroom`
Data controller: AppVita Studio - contact: appvitastudio@proton.me

## Short version

The Wrong Room is an offline observation and horror puzzle game. There is no account, no
sign-up and no server of ours. We do not receive, read or store anything about you or about
how you play. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising SDK (AdMob)
needs in order to show ads.

## What stays on your device

The game writes a small save file to the private storage of your phone. It contains:

- your progress: rooms cleared, stars, scores, best Endless run, Daily Challenge results,
  achievements, the anomalies you have found (Codex) and the notes you have read;
- your settings: language, music and sound volume, vibration, camera sensitivity, graphics
  quality, text size and accessibility options;
- whether an ad-free period earned with a rewarded video is still running.

Photos you take inside the game are pictures of the game's own rooms; they stay in memory for
the current room only and are never saved to your gallery or uploaded. There is no cloud sync,
no login, no online leaderboard and no analytics SDK. Nothing in the save file identifies you.
If you uninstall the app it is deleted with it, and you can erase it at any time from
Settings > Reset progress.

## Advertising (AdMob)

The Wrong Room is free and is paid for by ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google's SDK
may collect and process:

- the advertising identifier of the device (Advertising ID);
- approximate, coarse information derived from the IP address (such as country);
- technical information about the device and the app (model, operating system version, app
  version, language);
- interactions with the ads themselves (impressions, clicks).

This processing is done by Google as an independent controller. We do not receive this data and
we do not combine it with anything the game stores on your phone. Google's policies apply:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for
your consent before personalised ads are shown (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change
your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your
Advertising ID from the Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

No ad is ever shown while you are playing a room. Ads appear only in the menus and on the
result screens between rooms. Rewarded videos are always optional and always your choice.

## Permissions

- `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` - to load ads (the game itself works offline);
- `AD_ID` - the advertising identifier used by AdMob;
- `VIBRATE` - short vibrations as feedback (can be switched off in Settings).

The game does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call log or file access.

## Children

The Wrong Room is a psychological horror game and is not directed to children. It is intended
for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

## Your rights

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or
delete: everything is on your phone and under your control. For the data processed by Google
for advertising, please use Google's own controls linked above. In the EEA, the UK and
Switzerland the legal basis for the advertising processing is your consent where required,
and legitimate interest for non-personalised advertising and for the security of the service.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated. Material changes are also
described in the release notes of the app on Google Play.

## Contact

AppVita Studio - e-mail: appvitastudio@proton.me

# The Wrong Room - Informativa sulla privacy (italiano)

Ultimo aggiornamento: 24 settembre 2026

The Wrong Room e' un gioco horror di osservazione che funziona offline. Nessun account, nessun
server nostro, nessun SDK di analisi: non riceviamo e non conserviamo nulla su di te.

- **Sul dispositivo**: progressi, stelle, record, trofei, codice delle anomalie, biglietti letti
  e impostazioni sono salvati solo nella memoria privata del gioco. Le foto scattate nel gioco
  ritraggono le stanze del gioco e non vengono mai salvate in galleria ne' inviate. Tutto si
  cancella disinstallando il gioco o da Impostazioni > Azzera i progressi.
- **Pubblicita'**: il gioco e' gratuito e mostra annunci tramite Google AdMob, che puo' trattare
  l'ID pubblicita', informazioni tecniche sul dispositivo, la posizione approssimativa ricavata
  dall'indirizzo IP e le interazioni con gli annunci, secondo la Norma sulla privacy di Google
  (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Nello SEE, nel Regno Unito e in Svizzera il gioco chiede
  il consenso prima degli annunci personalizzati; la scelta si cambia da Impostazioni > Scelte
  privacy. Gli annunci non compaiono mai mentre giochi una stanza.
- **Permessi**: Internet e stato della rete (solo per gli annunci), ID pubblicita', vibrazione.
- **Minori**: il gioco non e' destinato ai minori di 13 anni.
- **Contatti**: appvitastudio@proton.me
