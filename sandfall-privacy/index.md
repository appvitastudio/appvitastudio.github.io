# Sand Fall: Color Flow - Privacy Policy

AppVita Studio - last updated 6 November 2026 - contact: appvitastudio@proton.me

Sand Fall: Color Flow ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android. It has no accounts, no login and no chat.

## Data the game keeps on your device
Your progress (levels, stars, records), settings (language, volume, theme) and statistics are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The free version may show ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are never shown while you play a level. To show ads, Google AdMob may collect and process: the advertising ID of your device, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google uses this data as described in its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Consent
In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form (UMP) before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

## Sharing
If you tap Share on a result, the game creates an image of the puzzle and opens the Android share sheet. Nothing is published automatically.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

## Data deletion
We do not hold any of your data on our servers. To delete the data kept by the game, uninstall it or clear its storage in Android settings. For data processed by Google AdMob, use your Google account and advertising ID settings, or write to us at appvitastudio@proton.me.

## Changes
We will update this page if the game changes how it handles data.
