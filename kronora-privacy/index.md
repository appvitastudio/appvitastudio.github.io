# Privacy Policy - Kronora

**Last updated: 29 September 2026**

Kronora ("the app") is a watch collection tracker published by **AppVita Studio**.
It helps you catalogue your watches, their value, service history, accuracy,
straps and how often you wear them. This policy explains what data the app
handles and why.

## 1. Your collection stays on your device

Everything you enter in the app - watches, brands and models, serial numbers,
purchase prices and valuations, insurance details, service records, accuracy
measurements, straps, wear log, wishlist, notes, and the photos and documents
(receipts, warranty cards) you attach - is stored **only on your device**, in the
app's private storage.

We do not collect it, we do not transmit it, we do not share it and we have no
servers that receive it. There is no account and no registration.

The only time this data leaves your device is when **you** choose to export or
share it (JSON backup, CSV export, PDF reports or the collection image, through
the Android share sheet): in that case you decide where it goes.

## 2. Photos and documents

When you add a photo or a document, the app asks Android to let you pick an
image. The selected image is resized and saved **inside the app, on your
device**. The app does not access the rest of your gallery and does not upload
any image anywhere.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Kronora is free and is supported by advertising provided by **Google AdMob**.

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and
  **approximate location derived from your IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller,
according to its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

**None of the collection data you enter in the app - watches, values, serial
numbers, photos or documents - is ever shared with the advertising network.**
The two things are kept completely separate.

You can watch an optional rewarded video to hide ads for 24 hours.

## 4. Consent (EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the
app shows you a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)
**before any ad is loaded**. You choose whether to allow personalised
advertising.

If consent is not given, no ads are requested at all.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from **Settings > Privacy
options (ads)** inside the app.

## 5. Notifications

The app can remind you when a service or a warranty expiry is coming up.
Reminders are scheduled **locally on your device**; no notification passes
through our servers, because we have none. You can refuse or revoke the
notification permission at any time in the Android settings.

## 6. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise
location, or any of your collection data. The app does not request the location
permission and uses no analytics SDK of its own.

## 7. Children

The app is not directed to children. It is intended for users aged 18 and over.

## 8. Your rights

Since your data never leaves your device, you are in full control of it:

- **Access and portability**: export your data at any time from Settings
  (JSON backup, CSV, PDF)
- **Deletion**: delete any item inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove
  everything
- **Advertising**: reset or delete your advertising ID from the Android settings,
  and change your consent from Settings > Privacy options (ads)

For anything concerning advertising data processed by Google, you can exercise
your rights directly with Google through the links in section 3.
