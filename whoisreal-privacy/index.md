# Privacy Policy - Who Is Real?

**Last updated: 21 October 2026**

Who Is Real? ("the game") is a psychological horror game published by **AppVita Studio**. This
policy explains what data the game handles and why.

## 1. Your progress stays on your device

Your game data - the night you are playing, your decisions, the notes and documents you have
collected, the endings you found and your settings (language, volumes, text size and speed,
reduced flashes, reduced distortion, jump scares, vibration) - is stored **only on your
device**, in the game's private storage.

We do not collect it, we do not transmit it, we do not share it and we have no servers that
receive it. There is no account and no registration, so there is no account to delete. The
story, the faces and the sounds are generated on your device: the game works fully offline.

**The surveillance cameras are fiction.** The "live feed", the scanner and the recordings are
part of the story: the game never uses your real camera or microphone.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Who Is Real? is free and is supported by advertising provided by **Google AdMob**:

- a banner on the menu screens only (never while you are playing a night)
- an occasional full-screen ad after an ending, when you go back to the menu (never while you are
  playing, at most one every four minutes)
- optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch to read a hint about an ending you have not
  found yet

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and
  **approximate location derived from your IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller, according to its
own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

**Your game progress and your decisions are never shared with the advertising network.**

## 3. Consent (EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows you
a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) **before any ad is loaded**. You
choose whether to allow personalised advertising.

No ad is requested until you have made a choice in that form. If you do not consent to
personalised advertising, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which are not
based on your past activity.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the **Privacy options** button in the
game's settings.

## 4. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location,
voice, or your game progress. The game does not request any special permission (no location,
camera, microphone, contacts or storage) and uses no analytics SDK of its own. It uses the
internet connection only for advertising and the consent form.

## 5. Children

The game is not directed to children. It is a psychological horror game intended for users aged
16 and over.

## 6. Your rights

Since your data never leaves your device, you are in full control of it:

- **Deletion**: use "Erase all progress" in the game's settings, clear the app data from the
  Android settings, or uninstall the game to remove everything
- **Advertising**: reset or delete your advertising ID from the Android settings, and change
  your consent from the Privacy options button in the game

For anything concerning advertising data processed by Google, you can exercise your rights
directly with Google using the links above.

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
