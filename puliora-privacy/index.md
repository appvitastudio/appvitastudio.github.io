# Privacy Policy - Puliora

**Last updated: 20 October 2026**

Puliora ("the app") is a text cleaner published by **AppVita Studio**. It finds and removes invisible
and unusual Unicode characters in text. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. It
applies to the Android app, the website https://appvitastudio.github.io/puliora/ and the Puliora
Chrome extension.

## 1. Your text stays on your device

Everything you paste, type, open, share to the app ("Share -> Puliora") or select with the
"Clean with Puliora" text menu is processed **only on your device**, inside the app.

We do not collect it, we do not transmit it, we do not share it and we have no servers that
receive it. There is no account and no registration, so there is no account to delete. Cleaning
works fully offline.

The app stores on your device only: your language and theme, custom rules if you press Save, and
an optional local history of cleanings (dates and counts only, never the text) that is off by
default. You can delete all of it from the app settings, by clearing the app data in the Android
settings, or by uninstalling the app.

When you choose "Replace selected text", the cleaned text is returned only to the app where you
selected it. When you choose Share or Download, the text or file goes only where you send it.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob) - Android app only

The Android app is free and is supported by a **banner ad** provided by **Google AdMob**. There are
no full-screen ads. The website has no ads.

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and
  **approximate location derived from your IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown, and diagnostic data

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller, according to its own
policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

**Your text is never shared with the advertising network.** The app uses the Internet permission
only for advertising.

## 3. Consent (EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows you a
consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) **before any ad is loaded**. You choose
whether to allow personalised advertising.

If consent is not given, no ads are requested at all.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the **Privacy options** button (shield
icon) at the top of the app.

## 4. Website and Chrome extension

The website processes text only in your browser: its pages block network requests from scripts
with a Content Security Policy, use no cookies and no analytics. The site is hosted on GitHub
Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site.

The Chrome extension reads the selected text of a tab only when you invoke it and the clipboard
only when you press its button. It sends nothing over the network and stores only your settings.

## 5. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location,
voice, or the text you clean. The app does not request any special permission (no location,
camera, microphone, contacts or storage) and uses no analytics SDK of its own.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

## 7. Your rights

Since your text never leaves your device, you are in full control of it:

- **Deletion**: use "Clear session" and the settings in the app, clear the app data from the
  Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything
- **Advertising**: reset or delete your advertising ID from the Android settings, and change your
  consent from the Privacy options button in the app

For anything concerning advertising data processed by Google, you can exercise your rights
directly with Google using the links above.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
