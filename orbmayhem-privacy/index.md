# Privacy Policy - Orb Mayhem: Physics Arena

**Last updated: 6 November 2026**

Orb Mayhem: Physics Arena ("the game") is published by **AppVita Studio**. This policy explains
what data the game handles.

## 1. No data leaves your device

Your game data - shards, unlocked items, your fighters, statistics, tournaments in progress,
saved replays and your settings (language, volumes, flashes, camera shake, graphics, vibration) -
is stored **only on your device**, in the game's private storage.

We do not collect it, we do not transmit it, we do not share it and we have no servers that
receive it. There is no account and no registration, so there is no account to delete.

The game works **fully offline**: it does not request the Internet permission, contains **no
advertising**, **no in-app purchases**, **no analytics** and **no third-party tracking SDK**.

## 2. Sharing images

When you tap "Share image" on a results screen, the game creates a picture of the battle result in
its private cache and opens the Android share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app in that
menu; what happens next is governed by the app you choose.

## 3. Permissions

The game only uses the vibration permission (for haptic feedback, which can be turned off in the
settings). It does not access location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

## 4. Children

The game contains abstract cartoon combat between geometric shapes, with no personal data
collection, no ads and no purchases.

## 5. Your rights

Since your data never leaves your device, you are in full control of it: use "Reset progress" in
the game's settings, clear the app data from the Android settings, or uninstall the game to remove
everything.

## 6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
