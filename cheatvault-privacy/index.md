
# Cheat Vault - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-27. Publisher: AppVita Studio. Contact: appvitastudio@proton.me

Cheat Vault ("the app") is an independent, unofficial reference app with cheat codes reported for Grand Theft Auto VI. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

## Data stored only on your device
- Your favorites, your personal unlock progress and your settings (console, language, accessibility options).
- They never leave the device unless you export a backup file yourself, to a location you choose.
- The app has no account and no login. It never asks for Rockstar, PlayStation or Xbox credentials.

## Network use
- Catalog updates: at start the app downloads a static public file (manifest.json and, if newer, the catalog) from https://appvitastudio.github.io/cheatvault-data/. No identifier is sent with this request. You can turn it off in Settings.
- Links to sources open in your browser only when you tap them.
- "Report a problem" opens your own email app with a prefilled text (cheat id, console, report type, app version, language). Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
- The app may show a banner ad provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK).
- AdMob may collect and process the advertising ID, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information and ad interaction data to show, measure and limit ads, as described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your Android settings.

## Permissions
Internet and network state (catalog updates and ads), and the advertising ID permission (ads). No location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or call log permission.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 and is intended for players of an adult-rated game.

## Data deletion
Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes all data kept by the app. There is no server copy.
