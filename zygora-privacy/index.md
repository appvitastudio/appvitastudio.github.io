# Privacy Policy - Zygora

**Last updated: 1 October 2026**

Zygora ("the game") is an idle evolution game published by
**AppVita Studio**. This policy explains what data the game handles and why.

## 1. Your progress stays on your device

Your game data - resources and generators, upgrades, eras, prestige progress
(Big Bang, Transcendence, Sanctuary), daily missions and streak, achievements,
statistics and your settings (language, sound, music, vibration, number notation) -
is stored **only on your device**, in the game's private storage. If you use the
backup feature, the backup code is created on your device and goes only where you
choose to paste or share it.

We do not collect it, we do not transmit it, we do not share it and we have no
servers that receive it. There is no account and no registration. The game works
fully offline.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Zygora is free and is supported by advertising provided by **Google AdMob**:
a banner at the bottom of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad after a
**Big Bang** (never more than once every few minutes), and optional rewarded
videos that you choose to watch to get boosts, time warps, chests or extra DNA.

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and
  **approximate location derived from your IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller,
according to its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

**Your game progress is never shared with the advertising network.**

## 3. Consent (EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the
game shows you a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)
**before any ad is loaded**. You choose whether to allow personalised
advertising.

If consent is not given, no ads are requested at all.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the **Privacy options
(ads)** button in the game's settings (Stats tab).

## 4. Notifications

The game can remind you when your chest is ready or your offline progress is full.
These reminders are scheduled **locally on your device**; nothing passes through
our servers, because we have none. You can refuse or revoke the notification
permission at any time in the Android settings.

## 5. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos,
precise location, or your game progress. Apart from notifications, the game does
not request any special permission (no location, camera, microphone, contacts or
storage) and uses no analytics SDK of its own.

## 6. Children

The game is not directed to children. It is intended for users aged 18 and over.

## 7. Your rights

Since your data never leaves your device, you are in full control of it:

- **Deletion**: clear the app data from the Android settings, or uninstall the
  game to remove everything
- **Advertising**: reset or delete your advertising ID from the Android settings,
  and change your consent from the Privacy options (ads) button in the game

For anything concerning advertising data processed by Google, you can exercise
your rights directly with Google through the links in section 2.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page changes
with it, and the new version is published at this same address.
