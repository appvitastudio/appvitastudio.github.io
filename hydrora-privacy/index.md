# Privacy Policy - Hydrora

**Last updated: 21 October 2026**

Hydrora ("the app") is a water tracker and drink reminder: it helps you log what you drink, set a
daily goal and receive optional reminders. It is published by **AppVita Studio**. This policy
explains what data the app handles and why.

## 1. Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter in Hydrora - the drinks you log (type, amount, time), your daily goal, your
custom drinks and glasses, your reminder schedule, milestones and settings (language, unit, theme) -
is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage.

We do not collect it, we do not transmit it, we do not share it and we have no servers that
receive it. There is no account and no registration, so there is no account to delete.

The app does not ask for your weight, age, sex or any health condition. The optional goal estimate
only uses the choices you make on screen (reference amount, activity, climate) and is computed on
the device.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
Hydrora is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

## 2. Notifications and widget

If you turn reminders on, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications. Reminders are
scheduled **locally** on your phone; no push service or server is involved.

The home-screen widget reads today's progress from the app's private storage on your device.
Drinks logged from the widget are saved on the device and added to your history the next time
the app opens.

## 3. Export, backup and deletion

You can export your entries (CSV) or a full backup (JSON) from Settings. The file is created on
your device and handed to the Android share menu: **you** choose where it goes (for example your
own cloud drive or e-mail). Hydrora never uploads it anywhere by itself.

You can delete all your data at any time from Settings > Your data > Delete all data, by clearing
the app data in the Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Hydrora is free and is supported by a small banner provided by **Google AdMob**. The banner is
shown only after the first setup, and the app never shows full-screen ads.

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and
  **approximate location derived from your IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller, according to its own
policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

**Your drinks, goals and settings are never shared with the advertising network.**

## 5. Consent (EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows you a
consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) **before any ad is loaded**. You choose
whether to allow personalised advertising.

If consent is not given, no ads are requested at all.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the **Ad privacy choices** item in the
app's Settings (shown when required in your region).

## 6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

## 7. Your rights

Since your data never leaves your device, you are in full control of it:

- **Access and portability**: export your data at any time (CSV or JSON)
- **Deletion**: delete single entries, use "Delete all data" in Settings, clear the app data from
  the Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything
- **Advertising**: reset or delete your advertising ID from the Android settings, and change your
  consent from the Ad privacy choices item in the app
