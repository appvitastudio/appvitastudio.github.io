# Privacy Policy - 100% Gap Finder

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy describes how the Android app "100% Gap Finder" (package com.appvitastudio.gapfinder), published by
AppVita Studio, handles information.

100% Gap Finder is an independent, unofficial companion application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

## Data you enter
Profiles, completion progress, counters, notes, favourites, personal goals, saved filters, the activity journal and
settings are stored only on your device. There is no account and no login. The app does not ask for or store
credentials of any game service, and it does not read your console, game account or save files. Android cloud backup
is disabled for this app.
You can export this data to a JSON or CSV file and import it again (More > Backup and restore). The file is saved only
where you choose.

## Catalog updates
The app can download a newer version of its public requirements catalog from https://appvitastudio.github.io over
HTTPS. This request contains no personal data beyond what any HTTPS request contains (such as your IP address, which
the hosting provider, GitHub, may log). Source links open in your browser only when you tap them.

## Advertising
The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob on the Home and Statistics screens. Google may collect and process
information such as the advertising ID, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information and ad
interaction data, to serve, measure and (with your consent) personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. See
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Consent
Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's User
Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in
More > Privacy options (ads consent). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

## Permissions
Internet and network state (catalog updates and ads) and the advertising ID permission. No location, contacts, SMS,
camera, microphone, call log, storage or notification permission is requested.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Deletion
Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored by it on your device. You can also delete a profile in
More > Save profiles.
