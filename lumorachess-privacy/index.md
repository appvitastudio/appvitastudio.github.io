# Privacy Policy - Lumora Chess: AI Chess

AppVita Studio - last updated: 10 November 2026 - contact: appvitastudio@proton.me

## Summary
The game works fully offline. It has no account, no login and no server of ours. Your games, statistics and settings are stored only on your device.

## Data stored on your device
Settings, finished games (PGN), statistics, puzzle and lesson progress. They never leave the device unless you choose to share or export a game (PGN/FEN) through the Android share sheet. Deleting the app or using "Delete all data" in Settings removes them.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (banner in menus, occasional full-screen ad after a finished game, optional rewarded video to unlock cosmetic themes). Ads are never shown during a game. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:
- the device advertising ID (Android advertising ID) and other device identifiers,
- approximate location derived from the IP address,
- app interaction and diagnostic data (ad impressions, clicks, crashes of the SDK),
for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Consent (UMP)
In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and where required by law, the app asks for your consent with the Google User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw consent at any time from Settings > Privacy > "Ad privacy options". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google > Ads).

## Children
The game is not directed to children under 13.

## Permissions
INTERNET and AD_ID are used only for ads and the consent form. VIBRATE is used for optional haptic feedback.
