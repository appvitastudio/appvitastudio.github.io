# Privacy Policy - Spoiler Shield

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy describes how the Android app "Spoiler Shield" (package com.appvitastudio.spoilershield), published by
AppVita Studio, handles information.

Spoiler Shield is an independent, unofficial companion app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by
Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

## Data you enter
Profiles, story progress, notes, favourites, checklist ticks, opened hints and recently viewed items are stored only on
your device. There is no account and no login. The app does not ask for or store credentials of any game service, and
it does not read your console, game account or save files. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app.
You can export this data to a JSON file and import it again (Settings > Backup and restore). The file is saved only
where you choose.

## Catalog updates
The app downloads its public guide catalog from https://appvitastudio.github.io over HTTPS. This request contains no
personal data beyond what any HTTPS request contains (such as your IP address, which the hosting provider, GitHub,
may log). External source links open in your browser only when you tap them.

## Advertising
The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process information such as the
advertising ID, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information and ad interaction data, to
serve, measure and (with your consent) personalise ads. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and
https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Consent
Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's User
Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in
Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

## Permissions
Internet and network state (catalog updates and ads) and the advertising ID permission. No location, contacts,
camera, microphone, storage or notification permission is requested.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Deletion
Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored by it on your device. You can also delete a profile in Settings >
Profiles.
