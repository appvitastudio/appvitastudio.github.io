# Neon Unlock: Arrow Puzzle - Privacy Policy

Developer: AppVita Studio - Contact: appvitastudio@proton.me
Last updated: 2026-11-11

## Summary
Neon Unlock is a puzzle game that works fully offline. It has no account and no sign-up. Your progress, stars, records, themes and settings are stored only on your device. AppVita Studio does not collect, receive or sell any personal data.

## Data stored on your device
Game progress, stars, records, daily puzzle results, unlocked themes and settings are saved locally on your phone (app storage). They never leave the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. You can also reset progress from Settings.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ads never appear during a puzzle. To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:
- the advertising ID of your device,
- approximate location derived from the IP address,
- device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics.
This data is processed by Google according to its policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Consent (UMP)
Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options. If you do not consent, ads may be limited or non-personalized. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

## Permissions
- Internet: only to load ads and the consent form. The game itself works offline.
- Vibration: haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings.
The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or files outside its own storage. Sharing a board image uses the Android share sheet only when you tap Share.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13.
