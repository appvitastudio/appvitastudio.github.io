# Privacy Policy - Roccara

**Last updated: 1 October 2026**

Roccara ("the game") is an offline strategy and city-building game published by
**AppVita Studio**. This policy explains what data the game handles and why.

## 1. Your game stays on your device

Your game data - villages, buildings and construction queues, army, raids,
research, eras, daily missions, the diary of what happened while you were away and
your settings (language, sound, notifications) - is stored **only on your device**,
in the game's private storage.

We do not collect it, we do not transmit it, we do not share it and we have no
servers that receive it. There is no account and no registration, so there is no
account to delete. The game works fully offline.

If you use the **backup** feature, the game creates a backup file on your device and
opens the Android share sheet: the file goes only where you choose to save or send
it. Importing a backup reads only the file you pick.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Roccara is free, has no in-app purchases and is supported by advertising provided
by **Google AdMob**:

- a banner at the bottom of some screens (never on the map)
- an occasional ad when you come back to the game, with a strict daily limit
- an occasional full-screen ad when you move to a new Era
- optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch to get a bonus (for example a
  shorter wait or extra loot)

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and
  **approximate location derived from your IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller,
according to its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

**Your game progress is never shared with the advertising network.**

## 3. Consent (EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the
game shows you a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)
**before any ad is loaded**. You choose whether to allow personalised
advertising.

If consent is not given, no ads are requested at all.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the **Privacy options**
button in the game's menu.

## 4. Notifications

The game can remind you about incoming attacks, finished construction queues,
returning raids and similar game events. These reminders are scheduled **locally on
your device**; nothing passes through our servers, because we have none. The game
asks for the notification permission only after your first raid, and you can refuse
or revoke it at any time in the Android settings.

## 5. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos,
precise location, or your game progress. Apart from notifications, the game does
not request any special permission (no location, camera, microphone or contacts)
and uses no analytics SDK of its own.

## 6. Children

The game is not directed to children. It is intended for users aged 18 and over.

## 7. Your rights

Since your data never leaves your device, you are in full control of it:

- **Deletion**: clear the app data from the Android settings, or uninstall the
  game to remove everything (backup files you exported stay where you saved them)
- **Advertising**: reset or delete your advertising ID from the Android settings,
  and change your consent from the Privacy options button in the game

For anything concerning advertising data processed by Google, you can exercise
your rights directly with Google through the links in section 2.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page changes
with it, and the new version is published at this same address.
