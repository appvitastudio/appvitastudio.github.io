# Privacy Policy - 100 Seconds

**Last updated: 15 October 2026**

100 Seconds ("the game") is a psychological horror game published by **AppVita Studio**.
This policy explains what data the game handles and why.

## 1. Your progress stays on your device

Your game data - nights played, endings and causes of death seen, story fragments found, the
tally of your nights, streaks, the last seed you played and your settings (language, volumes,
look sensitivity, field of view, graphics quality, brightness, accessibility options, subtitles,
vibration) - is stored **only on your device**, in the game's private storage.

We do not collect it, we do not transmit it, we do not share it and we have no servers that
receive it. There is no account and no registration, so there is no account to delete.
The game works fully offline.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

100 Seconds is free and is supported by advertising provided by **Google AdMob**:

- a banner on the menu screens (never during a night)
- an occasional full-screen ad between nights (never during the 100 seconds, never in your
  first nights of a session)
- an optional rewarded video that you choose to watch to reveal the rules of the night you
  just played

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and
  **approximate location derived from your IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller, according to its
own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

**Your game progress is never shared with the advertising network.**

## 3. Consent (EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows you
a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) **before any ad is loaded**. You
choose whether to allow personalised advertising. If you do not consent, Google may still show
non-personalised or limited ads: these still use the information described in section 2 (for
example the IP address, for approximate location and fraud prevention), but not to build an
advertising profile about you.

In these regions you can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the **Privacy options**
button in the game's settings.

## 4. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location,
voice, or your game progress. The game does not request any special permission (no location,
camera, microphone, contacts or storage) and uses no analytics SDK of its own.

## 5. Children and content

The game contains psychological horror (tension, sudden sounds, a threatening presence, no
blood). It is not directed to children and is intended for users aged 18 and over.

## 6. Your rights

Your game data never leaves your device, so you are in full control of it:

- **Deletion**: use "Reset progress" in the game's settings, clear the app data from the
  Android settings, or uninstall the game to remove everything
- **Advertising**: reset or delete your advertising ID from the Android settings, and change
  your consent from the Privacy options button in the game

For anything concerning advertising data processed by Google, you can exercise your rights
directly with Google using the links above.

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
