# Privacy Policy - Fish Evolve: Abyss Hunter

**Last updated: 12 November 2026**

Fish Evolve: Abyss Hunter ("the game") is an arcade game published by **AppVita Studio**.
This policy explains what data the game handles and why.

## 1. Your game data stays on your device

Your progress, saves, settings, stars, coins, unlocked fish and skins and statistics are stored
**only on your device**, in the game's private storage. We do not collect them, we do not
transmit them and we have no servers that receive them. There is no account and no
registration. The game works fully offline.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by advertising provided by **Google AdMob**.
Ads are never shown during a match. To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and
  **approximate location derived from your IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller,
according to its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

No game data (saves, progress, settings) is shared with the advertising network.

## 3. Consent (EU / EEA / UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the
game shows you a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform **before any
ad is loaded**. You choose whether to allow personalised advertising. If consent
is required and not given, no ads are requested at all.

You can change your choice at any time from **Settings > Privacy options** inside
the game.

## 4. Sharing screenshots

The Share button on the result screen creates an image of your match on your device
and opens the Android share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share
with. Nothing is ever posted automatically.

## 5. Permissions

- **Internet**: used only to load ads and the consent form. The game itself works
  fully offline.
- **Advertising ID**: used only by Google AdMob as described above.
- **Vibration**: optional haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings.

## 6. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos,
precise location or any content of your play. We use no analytics SDK of our own.

## 7. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## 8. Your rights

Since your game data never leaves your device, you are in full control of it:
uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage from Android Settings > Apps,
deletes all of it. For data processed by Google for advertising, see the Google
links above; you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID from Android Settings.

## 9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new
"Last updated" date.
