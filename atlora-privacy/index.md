# Atlora - Privacy Policy

Developer: AppVita Studio
Contact: appvitastudio@proton.me
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Atlora is an independent, unofficial companion app for video game players. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

## Data you create
Your checklist progress, favourites, notes, personal pins, personal tasks and settings are stored only on your device. Atlora has no account system and no servers that receive this data. We cannot see, access or recover it.

You can export a backup file (JSON or CSV). The file is saved where you choose and is never uploaded by the app. Android system backup (if enabled on your device) may include this app's local data according to your Google account settings.

## Permissions
- Internet and network state: used only to download catalog updates (a public, read-only file) and, where enabled, to show ads.
- Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by Google AdMob, where ads are enabled.

Atlora does NOT request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call log or file storage permissions. The atlas is a schematic diagram and does not use GPS.

## Catalog updates
The app may download a catalog file from a static public address (appvitastudio.github.io). The request contains no personal data beyond what any web request includes (such as your IP address, processed by the hosting provider, GitHub, under its own policy).

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
Where ads are enabled, the app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information and ad interactions, to show and measure ads and, with your consent, personalised ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, consent is requested through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are loaded. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from Settings > Privacy and ad consent options in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Children
Atlora is not directed to children under 13.

## Data deletion
All data is on your device: uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. There is no account to delete.

## Changes
We will update this page if the app's data practices change.
