# Hockora - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026
App: Hockora: Air Hockey Neon Duel (Android) - package `com.appvitastudio.hockora`
Data controller: AppVita Studio - contact: appvitastudio@proton.me

**Hockora** ("the game") is an air hockey game. This policy explains what data the game handles.

## Data the game stores on your device
Your progress (level, XP, career stars, trophies, statistics, unlocked items), your settings (sound, music,
vibration, language, visual options) and the results of the Daily Match and Survival are saved **only on your
device**. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

The game works offline. It has no account, no login, no chat, no online multiplayer, and it does not ask for
access to contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone. The 2-player mode is played on the same device.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The game is free and shows ads through **Google AdMob** (never during a match). To show and measure ads, Google
may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app
information, and interactions with ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you
are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown (Google User Messaging Platform), and you can change your
choice at any time from **Settings > Privacy choices** in the game.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children
The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are requested with
Google's standard settings.
