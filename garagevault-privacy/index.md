# Privacy Policy - Garage Vault

**Last updated: 26 November 2026**

Garage Vault ("the app") is an independent companion app published by **AppVita Studio**. It lets you record, on your
own device, the vehicles you own in a video game, your garages, a wishlist, customizations, notes and photos.

Garage Vault is **independent and unofficial**. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Rockstar Games or
Take-Two Interactive. It cannot read your console, your game saves or your Rockstar account.

## 1. Your collection stays on your device

Everything you enter - garages, vehicles, colors, customizations, notes, tags, wishlist, history, statistics and the
photos you choose - is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage.

There is no account and no registration. We have no servers that receive your collection, and we do not collect, sell
or share it. Your data leaves the device only when **you** choose to export a backup or share a vehicle card through the
Android file picker or share sheet.

## 2. Photos

Photos are selected with the Android Photo Picker: the app does not need permission to read your whole gallery. A copy
of each photo you pick is resized and saved in the app's private storage. Photos are never uploaded and are never
included in the public vehicle catalog.

## 3. Backups

- **Manual backup**: you can export a JSON, ZIP (with photos) or CSV file. **These files are NOT encrypted** and may
  contain your notes and photos: store them in a safe place. We never receive them.
- **Android backup**: if enabled on your phone, Android may back up the app's data to your Google account. You can turn
  it off in the Android settings.

## 4. Vehicle catalog updates

When the app starts (and you are online), it downloads the public vehicle catalog from our static website
(https://appvitastudio.github.io). This is a read-only download of a public file: **no personal data and no data from
your collection is sent**. As with any website, the hosting provider (GitHub) may process your IP address in its
server logs. You can turn automatic updates off in Settings.

## 5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is supported by banner advertising provided by **Google AdMob**. Banners appear only in a few
non-critical places (home, catalog, statistics); never while you add a vehicle, fill in a form, or back up / restore.

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and **approximate location derived from your
  IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller, according to its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

**Nothing from your collection is ever shared with the advertising network.**

## 6. Consent (EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form from Google's
User Messaging Platform (UMP) **before any ad is loaded**. If consent cannot be obtained, no ads are requested. You can
change or withdraw your choice at any time from **Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy options** inside the app. You can also
reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings.

## 7. Permissions

- **Internet and network state**: to download the public vehicle catalog and to load ads.
- **Advertising ID** (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call log or storage permissions.

## 8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## 9. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored by it on your device. You can also delete single vehicles, garages and
wishlist entries inside the app.
