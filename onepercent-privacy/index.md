# Privacy Policy - 1%

**Last updated: 15 October 2026**

1% ("the game") is a psychological horror game published by **AppVita Studio**. This policy
explains what data the game handles and why.

## 1. Your progress stays on your device

Your game data - the checkpoint of your current night, the endings you found, the clues you
discovered, the photos and recordings you take inside the game, statistics and your settings
(language, volumes, subtitles, darkness calibration, graphics quality, accessibility options,
controls, vibration) - is stored **only on your device**, in the game's private storage.

We do not collect it, we do not transmit it, we do not share it and we have no servers that
receive it. There is no account and no registration, so there is no account to delete. The
game works fully offline.

**The in-game phone is fiction.** Its camera, voice recorder, messages and calls are part of the
story: the game never uses your real camera, microphone, contacts, messages or phone line, and
never sends anything outside the game.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

1% is free and is supported by advertising provided by **Google AdMob**:

- a banner on the menu screens (never during the night)
- an occasional full-screen ad after an ending, when you go back to the menu (never during the
  night, at most one every few minutes)
- optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch to read a hint

Energy (the "1%" battery) is never sold and never given by ads.

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

- the **advertising identifier** of your device (Android Advertising ID)
- technical data such as device model, operating system version, language and
  **approximate location derived from your IP address**
- data about interaction with the ads shown

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent data controller, according to its
own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and user consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

**Your game progress is never shared with the advertising network.**

## 3. Consent (EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows you
a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) **before any ad is loaded**. You
choose whether to allow personalised advertising.

If consent is not given, no ads are requested at all.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the **Privacy options** button in the
game's settings.

## 4. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location,
voice, or your game progress. The game does not request any special permission (no location,
camera, microphone, contacts or storage) and uses no analytics SDK of its own.

## 5. Children

The game is not directed to children. It is a horror game intended for users aged 16 and over.

## 6. Your rights

Since your data never leaves your device, you are in full control of it:

- **Deletion**: use "Reset progress" in the game's settings, clear the app data from the
  Android settings, or uninstall the game to remove everything
- **Advertising**: reset or delete your advertising ID from the Android settings, and change
  your consent from the Privacy options button in the game

For anything concerning advertising data processed by Google, you can exercise your rights
directly with Google using the links above.

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
